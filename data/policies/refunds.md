# Refund policy
Customers can request a refund for a delivered item within 30 days of delivery.
Damaged or defective items are refunded in full within 30 days of delivery; no return shipping cost for the customer.
Change-of-mind returns are refunded within 14 days of delivery once the item is returned unused.
Refunds above 200 EUR always require approval by a human support agent.
Orders that were already refunded cannot be refunded again.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.
