# Shipping and late delivery policy
Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days after the order ships.
If an order is more than 5 days past its expected delivery date, the customer may choose a full refund or a free re-shipment.
If an order is late by 5 days or less, we apologise, share the tracking status, and ask the customer to wait.
Orders in "processing" status have not shipped yet and can be cancelled free of charge.
